Status

What is proven on the boards today, and what is not.

OpenGL ES 2.0 conformance

We run the Khronos conformance tests (dEQP-GLES2, the 17,165 cases of the "mustpass" list) on Zephyr, on the real GPU.

Raspberry Pi 42026-10-01firmware tryboot17,07525623
  • The two Verdin rows were measured with kexec (Zephyr started from the running Linux). That is a development path, not a bootloader boot. They are being measured again from U-Boot.
  • Raspberry Pi 4: all 25 failures are cube cases where the picture differs from the ES 2.0 reference. No other case fails.
  • iMX8M Plus: the run covered 17,371 cases, more than the mustpass list. Limited to the list, some pass and not-supported counts are known only as a range.
  • "Not supported" cases need a feature we do not offer yet (for example 4x multisampling in some images, or clip control).

Booted from the bootloader

  • The one Slint sample passes its picture check on all three boards, booted from U-Boot (Verdins) or the Pi firmware (Pi 4).
  • Verdin AM62P: the GL test suite gives the same result per case from U-Boot as from kexec: 37 of 38 cases pass on both; the one failing case is a known ES 3 case.
  • The one triangle sample draws a bit-identical picture on all three boards, booted from U-Boot (Verdins) or the Pi firmware (Pi 4).
  • Verdin iMX8M Plus: the triangle gives the same picture record from U-Boot as from kexec.
  • The watchdog brings each board back to Linux, proven on each board.

Not yet

  • Full dEQP-GLES2 runs on the Verdins from U-Boot.
  • The cluster demo on the iMX8M Plus.
  • On the AM62P, splitting the GPU's fragment phase further needs the firmware's own timestamps (HWPerf); see Demos, "Where the PowerVR's frame time goes".
  • On the iMX8M Plus, finding the per-frame floor of about 24 ms in glmark2-es2.
  • Touch input.
  • A board that boots Zephyr by default, without Linux (written down for the Pi 4, not done).
  • OpenGL ES 3.x: later. ES 2.0 comes first. One early run of dEQP-GLES3 on the AM62P (2026-09-26) passed 31,278 of 42,810 cases (73.1%).

Known issues

  • iMX8M Plus: in one GL test case, a 1 px point draw sometimes never reaches the surface (about half the runs of that case). It is being investigated.
