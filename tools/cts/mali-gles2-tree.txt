# gles2-mustpass.txt is selected by exactly one line.
# Sessions: S1 everything but functional.shaders and functional.fragment_ops;
# S2 fragment_ops and shaders.operator; S3 shaders random, matrix, swizzles,

# fault on other boards (loops, indexing, random, texture, fbo.render, draw)
# stand alone. The hang-risk cases of docs/notes/mali-gles2-forecast.md (the 208
# fragment loops with a dynamic or uniform bound) are the last six rows of S3,
